Echo Hunt

A hidden beacon is somewhere on a grid. Listen for its ping, then walk to it. You do not need to see the screen.

How to play

Wear stereo headphones. Move with the arrow keys or W, A, S, D. Press Space to ping. A ping in your left ear means the beacon is west of you. Right ear means east. A high pitch means it is north. A low pitch means it is south. A louder ping means closer. Press H for a hint, and R to restart the level. Reach the beacon to win. Each level adds more squares.
