Echo Hunt: The Sunken Vault

An audio adventure. Play with headphones and your eyes closed.

Your light has failed deep inside a flooded vault. Find the lost relic, carry it to the exit gate, and escape. Sound is all you have. Something in the dark is listening for you.

How to play

You are in a maze of stone passages. Move with the arrow keys or W, A, S, D. Walls stop you with a thud, and after each step you hear which ways are open.

The beacon guides you. A ping in your left ear means the target is west, right ear means east. High pitch means north, low pitch means south, louder means closer. Beware: walls are in the way, so the shortest line is not always a path.

First find the relic, which shimmers. Then find the exit gate, which rings like a bell. From level 2, the Warden prowls. Its growl gets louder as it nears, and a heartbeat means it is close. Three lives. Press Space to ping, Q for an echo scan that measures each open corridor, H for a hint, T for status, I for position, R to restart the level, and ? for help.
